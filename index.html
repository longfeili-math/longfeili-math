<h2>About</h2>
<p>
I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from Vanderbilt University in 2026
under the supervision of Professor Akram Aldroubi.
I am currently a tenure-track faculty member at
Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT).
</p>
<h2>Research</h2>
<p>
My research interests include applied and computational harmonic analysis,
sampling theory, signal processing, and inverse problems.
</p>
