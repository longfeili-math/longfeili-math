About Me

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from Kansas State University in 2026, under the supervision of Professor Yan Soibelman.

Before joining Kansas State University, I obtained my M.Sc. and B.Sc. in Mathematics both from Beijing Normal University.

Research Interests

My research lies at the intersection of algebra, geometry, and mathematical physics. I am particularly interested in homological mirror symmetry and related topics, including:

  • Fukaya–Seidel categories and their algebraic models
  • Algebra of the infrared and its geometric generalizations
  • A∞- and L∞-structures, operads, and deformation theory

My current research focuses on the algebra of the infrared with curve-valued potentials, its relationship with Fukaya–Seidel categories, and higher-dimensional generalizations involving Ed-algebras.

I am also interested in the interplay between geometry and representation theory, particularly cohomological Hall algebras and their connections to mathematical physics.
