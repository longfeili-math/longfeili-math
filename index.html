About

I am a mathematician working in symplectic geometry, homological mirror symmetry, and higher algebra.

My research focuses on Fukaya--Seidel categories, algebras of the infrared, A-infinity and L-infinity structures, and related questions in mirror symmetry.

Research

My current research concerns the algebra of the infrared for curve-valued potentials and its relation with Fukaya--Seidel categories.
