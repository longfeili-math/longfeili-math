About Me

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from Kansas State University in 2026, under the supervision of Professor Yan Soibelman.

Research Interests

My research lies at the intersection of algebra, geometry, and mathematical physics, with a particular interest in Fukaya–Seidel categories.

  • Homological mirror symmetry and Fukaya–Seidel categories
  • Algebra of the infrared and its geometric generalizations
  • A∞- and L∞-structures, operads, and deformation theory
  • Cohomological Hall algebras and their connections to representation theory

My current research focuses on extending the algebra of the infrared to curve-valued potentials, its relationship with Fukaya–Seidel categories, and higher-dimensional generalizations involving Ed-algebras.
